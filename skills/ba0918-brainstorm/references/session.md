# Session

Establish the desired outcome, why it matters, scope, exclusions, and constraints. Split a broad
request into independently valuable ordered units, obtain agreement on the map, and detail only the
first unit.

Ask one open question about the next material decision. Do not turn discovery into a checklist of
small approvals or ask settled questions again. Challenge both the human's proposal and your own
with counterexamples. Distinguish confirmed facts, inferences, and unverified hypotheses.

Record semantic changes as agreements, prohibitions, unresolved matters and their owner, explicitly
delegated choices and reasons, rejected options and reasons, or revisions replacing earlier items.
Save only after the human agrees that meaning changed.
